The survey is small, six qualified responses, so it’s directional, but it says the same thing. Every respondent searched at least three times before deciding, two searched more than ten times. Average trust in an app’s percentage is just 1.7 out of 5. Four of six said the hardest part was too many options or too many apps. And price came sixth out of seven: journey time, confirmation and arrival time matter more. Interesting tension with the interviews: people use the percentage as a rule, but they don’t really trust it. That’s exactly why it needs to be honest and explained.
