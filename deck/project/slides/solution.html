Two solutions, one plan the user believes

A · Fixes comparison

Best Way to Reach

  • One ranked list: trains, classes, dates, nearby stations, bus, flight
  • Chips plus a “my cut-off” filter, 80% by default
  • Top pick with a reason, plus a confirmed backup

B · Fixes trust

Honest Outcome Meter

  • Own berth / RAC / stays WL instead of one “% confirm”
  • “Last 10 similar Saturdays” shows why
  • Never says guaranteed; ranges when data is thin
From those ideas I shortlisted two solutions that work together. Best Way to Reach fixes comparison: one ranked list across every alternative, priority chips, and a top pick with a reason plus a confirmed backup. The Honest Outcome Meter fixes trust: it splits the prediction into own berth, RAC and stays waitlisted, explains it with past outcomes, and never says guaranteed. Trade-off: honest odds may look less positive short term, but they build the trust that drives repeat use.
