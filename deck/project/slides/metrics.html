One North Star, held in check by guardrails

26% → 42%

24-hour resolved booking rate for users who hit WL or no seats

Supporting

  • 60% of WL users open Best Way to Reach
  • Searches before booking: 6 → 3
  • Time to booking: −40%
  • Cross-mode bookings: +10 pts

Guardrails

  • Regret (“wrong choice”) ≤ 8%
  • Predicted vs actual CNF / RAC / WL within 5 pts
  • No rise in cancellations of picks
  • “Prediction wrong” complaints −30%
The North Star is the 24-hour resolved booking rate, from 26 to 42 percent. Leading indicators: how many waitlisted users open Best Way to Reach, fewer searches before booking, faster time to booking, and more cross-mode bookings. And guardrails, because a recommendation can be wrong: post-trip regret, calibration of predicted versus actual CNF, RAC and WL, cancellations, and complaints about wrong predictions. Every event is in the tracking spec in the PRD.
