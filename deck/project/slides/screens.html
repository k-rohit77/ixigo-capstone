The prototype in four screens

Live: best-way-to-reach.lovable.app

1 · Honest odds

2 · Ranked list

3 · Odds + backup

4 · Plan set

Here’s the prototype, live on Lovable. Screen one: the search result shows the outcome meter on a WL 42 train and says it’s below the user’s 80% cut-off, then offers “See best ways to reach.” Screen two: one ranked list with priority chips, the cut-off filter, a top pick with its reason and a confirmed backup. Long shots, including the user’s own train, move to one line at the bottom, so nothing is hidden. Screen three: the detail sheet explains the odds with the last ten similar Saturdays, keeps a backup ready, and states protection terms in plain words. Screen four: the plan is set, and after the trip we ask one question, “Was this the right choice?”, which feeds the ranking. [Switch to best-way-to-reach.lovable.app and click through.]
