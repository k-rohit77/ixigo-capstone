I ran 15 interviews over WhatsApp and phone, in English and Hindi, across four segments: people who resolved on ixigo, people who left for IRCTC, a bus or an agent, people who gave up, and people who don’t use ixigo at all. I also ran an 18-question bilingual survey, compared five competitors, and tested seven hypotheses.
