Go-to-market is in-product: the entry point appears only when someone hits a waitlist, so there’s no extra marketing cost and no discounts. We pilot with 5% of waitlisted users on the 20 busiest routes as an A/B test, then families, then everyone, then the PNR page for people already holding a waitlisted ticket. Each step has a clear exit criterion. The growth loop: every trip outcome makes the odds and ranking sharper, which builds trust and brings users back first.
