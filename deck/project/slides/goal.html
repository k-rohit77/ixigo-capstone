Resolved booking = a confirmed train, bus or flight for the trip, or a waitlisted ticket with a backup or protection the user chose knowingly.

This is the number the whole project is about. Today, of every 100 users whose preferred train shows waitlisted or no seats, only 26 complete a resolved booking within 24 hours. The goal is 42 in six months. Resolved means a confirmed train, bus or flight, or a waitlisted ticket where the user deliberately chose a backup or protection. A plain WL ticket with no plan does not count.
