Some context. ixigo has about 85 million monthly users and booked over 100 million train passenger segments last year. Trains bring in about 511 crore, the biggest slice of revenue, and 85% of transactions are repeat. Through ConfirmTkt and AbhiBus, ixigo already has predictions, alternate trains and buses. So the opportunity isn’t building inventory; it’s helping users decide.
