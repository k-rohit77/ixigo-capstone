I spoke to 20 travellers, 20 calls and one WhatsApp chat. Three patterns stood out. First, 8 of them have a personal cut-off: they book a waitlisted ticket only above 80 to 95 percent. The number is their decision rule, so it has to be honest. Second, 3 were let down by high odds: they often got RAC, or a high-chance ticket didn’t confirm, just like my own 90 percent ticket that ended in RAC. Third, priorities differ: comfort, punctuality, speed, safety, timing. One sort order can’t serve all of them. I also heard that 8 already think about flights, buses or Tatkal as a fallback, 7 book mainly on IRCTC or MakeMyTrip, and 3X Refund terms were unclear to several people. These are qualitative signals, not market numbers.
