Primary barrier

Users don’t lack options. They lack a way to choose with confidence.

They can’t compare trains, dates, buses and flights in one view, and a single “% confirm” hides whether they’ll get a berth, RAC or nothing.

So the primary barrier is low decision confidence. Not inventory, which is out of scope and usually exists. Not mainly price. It’s two things together: users can’t compare alternatives on what matters to them, and they don’t trust the certainty they’re shown. Both are fixable inside the app.
