# Interview Guide – WhatsApp & Call

Use the **call** version wherever possible (15–20 min). Use the **WhatsApp** version only when someone can't talk.

---

## A. Call interview (15–20 min)

### Opening (say this)
"Thanks for your time! I'm doing a short research project on train travel. There are no right or wrong answers, and I'm not selling anything. Can I take notes? Can I quote you without your name?"

### 1. Warm-up (2 min)
1. How often do you travel by train? / आप कितनी बार ट्रेन से जाते हैं?
2. Which app do you usually book on, and why that one? / आप आमतौर पर किस ऐप से बुक करते हैं, और क्यों?

### 2. Last waitlisted trip (5 min)
3. Tell me about the last time your train showed WL or no seats. Where were you going, when, and who was with you? / पिछली बार जब ट्रेन में WL या सीट नहीं मिली, तब कहाँ जा रहे थे, कब, और किसके साथ?
4. How many days before the journey were you booking? / यात्रा से कितने दिन पहले बुक कर रहे थे?
5. What was the WL number, and did the app show a chance of confirmation? / WL नंबर क्या था, और क्या ऐप ने कन्फ़र्म होने की संभावना दिखाई?

### 3. What they did next (4 min)
6. What did you do right after seeing WL? Walk me through it step by step. / WL देखने के बाद आपने क्या किया? स्टेप बाय स्टेप बताइए।
7. Did you check other trains, dates, nearby stations, bus or flight? Which ones, and in which app? / क्या दूसरी ट्रेन, तारीख, पास का स्टेशन, बस या फ़्लाइट देखी? किस ऐप में?
8. How many times did you search before deciding? How long did it take overall? / फ़ैसला करने से पहले कितनी बार सर्च किया? कुल कितना समय लगा?
9. Did you try Tatkal? What happened? / क्या तत्काल ट्राई किया? क्या हुआ?
10. What was the most frustrating moment? / सबसे ज़्यादा परेशानी किस पल हुई?

### 4. Final decision (4 min)
11. What did you finally do, and where did you book? / आख़िर में क्या किया, और कहाँ से बुक किया?
12. Why that option? *(Ask "why?" again on the answer.)* / वही विकल्प क्यों चुना?
13. What did you give up to get it: money, time, comfort, station? / इसके लिए क्या छोड़ना पड़ा: पैसा, समय, आराम, स्टेशन?
14. If you booked WL, what was your backup if it didn't confirm? / अगर WL बुक किया, तो कन्फ़र्म न होने पर बैकअप क्या था?
15. Did anyone help you decide (family, agent, friend)? What did they tell you? / क्या किसी ने फ़ैसले में मदद की? उन्होंने क्या कहा?

### 5. Trust in prediction (2 min)
16. Do you trust the "% chance to confirm" apps show? Why or why not? / ऐप जो "% कन्फ़र्म चांस" दिखाते हैं, क्या उस पर भरोसा है? क्यों?
17. Has a high-chance ticket ever ended as RAC or stayed WL? How did you feel about the app then? / क्या कभी ज़्यादा चांस वाला टिकट RAC या WL रह गया? तब ऐप के बारे में क्या लगा?
18. Have you used ixigo's Alternate Travel Plan, Assured, or IRCTC VIKALP? Did it help? / क्या ixigo Alternate Travel Plan, Assured या IRCTC VIKALP इस्तेमाल किया? मदद मिली?

### 6. Ideal fix (2 min)
19. If an app gave you one ready plan plus a backup, what must it show before you'd book it? / अगर ऐप एक तैयार प्लान और बैकअप दे, तो बुक करने से पहले उसमें क्या दिखना ज़रूरी है?
20. If you could change one thing in train booking apps, what would it be? / ट्रेन बुकिंग ऐप में एक चीज़ बदल सकें, तो क्या बदलेंगे?

### Closing
"Thank you! Do you know 1–2 people who faced a waitlisted ticket recently and could talk to me for 15 minutes?"

## B. WhatsApp version (send in 3 messages)

**Message 1 – Context**
> Hi! Thanks for helping 🙏 Just 8 quick questions about your last train trip where the ticket was waitlisted or had no seats. Voice notes are totally fine. Hindi or English, whatever is easy!

**Message 2 – The trip**
> 1. Where were you going, when, and with how many people?
> 2. What did the app show (WL number / % chance)?
> 3. What did you do next? Other trains, dates, bus, flight, Tatkal, agent?
> 4. How many times did you search, and how long did it take to decide?

**Message 3 – Decision & trust**
> 5. What did you finally book, where, and why that option?
> 6. Did you trust the "% chance to confirm"? Has a high-chance ticket ever ended as RAC or stayed WL?
> 7. What was the most frustrating part?
> 8. If an app gave you one ready plan plus a backup, what would it need to show for you to book it?

*Tip: follow up on any short answer with "why?" or "can you tell me more?"*

---

## C. Probing lines (use anytime)
- "Why?" (ask twice)
- "Can you tell me more about that?"
- "What happened next?"
- "How did that feel?"
- "Can you show me a screenshot?"

**Don't:** suggest answers, pitch your solution, or ask "would you use X?".
